# Website theme explorations

Generated on 2026-08-14 as high-fidelity desktop homepage studies. These are
visual references, not implementation assets, and nothing in this directory is
part of the deployed `site/` publication.

## Shared prompt frame

Each study presents a personal homepage for Henry Bissonnette, an AI engineer
and wide-ranging intellectual writer. The shared subject is the convergence of
intelligence, life, computation, nature, and transcendence. Every prompt asked
for a functional, readable, art-directed 16:10 desktop webpage rather than a
device mockup, with the following recurring copy:

- `HENRY BISSONNETTE`
- `ESSAYS  PROJECTS  NOTES  ABOUT`
- `INTELLIGENCE • LIFE • TRANSCENDENCE`
- `Toward living intelligence.`
- `Essays and experiments in artificial intelligence, computation, nature, and the long human ascent.`
- `THE WOMB PRINCIPLE`
- `LIFE AS TECHNOLOGY`

Across the set, the prompts explicitly avoided cyberpunk, retrofuturism,
robots, glowing brains, circuit-board leaves, Matrix code, sci-fi HUDs,
generic SaaS cards, and the familiar nature-versus-technology split image.

## Directions

1. **The Neural Grove** (`01-neural-grove.png`)
   A nocturnal mineral-black editorial page in which mycelia, neurons,
   capillaries, roots, and star maps become one delicate bioluminescent
   organism. Bone-white typography, amber life, and restrained cyan signals.

2. **Generative Botany** (`02-generative-botany.png`)
   A warm-white museum-catalog page centered on a translucent form between a
   seed, embryo, folded protein, and topological surface. Mathematical
   construction lines treat code and life as descriptions of one process.

3. **The Computational Terrarium** (`03-computational-terrarium.png`)
   A deep forest-black digital habitat viewed through layers of glass, where
   moss, fluid networks, colonies, roots, and graphs form a self-sustaining
   ecology of ideas.

4. **The Monastic Machine** (`04-monastic-machine.png`)
   An austere carbon-black field organized around an incomplete mathematical
   ring. Rare-book typography and precise annotations present technology as
   disciplined attention directed toward the transcendent.

5. **Living Systems Atlas** (`05-living-systems-atlas.png`)
   A saturated ultramarine computational notebook whose primary diagram evolves
   from source code to neural network, vascular system, roots, cells, and
   embryonic morphology. Exact, energetic, and engineering-forward.

6. **Chromatic Morphogenesis** (`06-chromatic-morphogenesis.png`)
   A luminous peach field where coral and ultraviolet cellular territories
   differentiate across a particle boundary. The page itself appears to be
   growing into a living organism: joyful, radical, and post-digital.

7. **Cellular Archipelago** (`07-cellular-archipelago.png`)
   Chromatic Morphogenesis expanded into many parallel cultures. Irregular
   coral, cobalt, violet, moss, ochre, and turquoise territories bud, merge,
   press against one another, recede, and leave open clearings for thought.
   The prior callout indicators are removed entirely.

8. **The Little Wild World** (`08-little-wild-world.png`)
   The terrarium made intimate and genuinely inhabited. Moss, fungi, water,
   roots, spores, a concealed salamander, luminous beetle, snail, and resting
   moth make computation feel nurtured rather than engineered.

9. **River Intelligence** (`09-river-intelligence.png`)
   A living current of membranes, filaments, organisms, particles, and cellular
   colonies, expressing participation rather than mastery. Sparse dark threads
   gather into a vivid, self-organizing downstream ecology.

## Chromatic Morphogenesis page family

After selecting direction six, five additional studies apply its design system
to different kinds of real website content. The shared invariants are the open
peach field, coral-to-ultraviolet cellular territories phasing into graphs,
bold black typography, colored navigation dots, and black multicolor topic bar.

10. **Essay reader** (`10-essay-reader.png`)
    A text-first reading page for *Life Is the First Technology*. Cellular and
    graph growth stay entirely in the margins around a narrow serif text column.

11. **Essays and notes archive** (`11-essays-notes-archive.png`)
    A dense but open editorial index with subject filters, dates, titles,
    categories, and descriptions organized as rows rather than cards.

12. **Selected systems** (`12-selected-systems.png`)
    A projects index for Living File System, Womb, and Cultural Dynamics Lab.
    Each project is a substantial typographic band framed by a distinct living
    territory.

13. **Living File System case study** (`13-lfs-case-study.png`)
    A technical project page in which coral cellular intent becomes a directed
    graph through design, tracks, and agents, then gathers into violet living
    software. Here the graph edges carry actual architectural meaning.

14. **Cultural Dynamics Lab** (`14-cultural-dynamics-lab.png`)
    A functional simulator layout with controls, an interactive multi-culture
    cellular field, a time-series graph, and live culture/diversity summaries.

## Generation mode

The first six studies were produced through six separate calls to the built-in
image generation tool using the `ui-mockup` prompt taxonomy. No reference
images were used. Studies seven through nine were produced through three more
separate built-in calls: seven used six as a mood reference, eight used three,
and nine synthesized the values of one, three, and six without editing them.
Studies ten through fourteen were produced through five separate built-in calls
using six as a strict design-system reference.
