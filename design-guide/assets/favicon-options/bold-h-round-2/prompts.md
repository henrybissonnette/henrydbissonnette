## 01 — Three-shape monolith

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square favicon concept for HenryBissonnette.com, designed first for 16x16 and 32x32 browser-tab display
Primary request: Create a radically simplified successor to the Living H reference: one massive uppercase H built from exactly three flat shapes — one coral-red vertical slab, one ultraviolet-blue vertical slab, and one thick golden-orange rectangular bridge. The H must be unmistakable before any symbolism is noticed.
Input images: Image 1 is the earlier Living H concept; preserve only its H idea and bilateral color logic, simplify it by at least tenfold. Image 2 is the website reference; use only its warm cream, coral, gold, violet, and cobalt palette.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat warm peach-cream square.
Style/medium: ultra-bold vector-friendly identity mark, poster-like flat pigment, hard clean edges.
Composition/framing: centered H occupying 82 percent of the square; strokes extremely thick; large counters; generous but compact outer margin; designed as though snapped to a 16-pixel grid.
Constraints: exactly one uppercase H; exactly three colored foreground shapes; no text besides the H glyph; no circles, cells, dots, lines, particles, texture, gradients, shadows, highlights, outlines, bevels, 3D, mockup, or watermark. Strong silhouette and high contrast at 16 pixels.
Avoid: thin crossbar, granular construction, decorative detail, corporate tech logo, sports monogram, circuit board, DNA, app-button gloss.
```

## 04 — Grown split H

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square favicon concept for HenryBissonnette.com, designed first for 16x16 and 32x32 browser-tab display
Primary request: Create a single solid block-uppercase H silhouette split vertically into two flat living color territories: saturated coral-vermilion on the left and ultraviolet-cobalt on the right. Give the outer H silhouette two or three broad, shallow organic bites or bulges total — never small bubbles — so it feels grown rather than typeset while remaining instantly legible.
Input images: Image 1 is the earlier Living H concept; keep the letter and opposing cultures but compress all complexity into the silhouette. Image 2 supplies palette only.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly flat warm peach-cream square.
Style/medium: ultra-simple flat emblem, vector-friendly, graphic, iconic, unconventional.
Composition/framing: centered H fills 84 percent of square; heavy strokes; wide counters; one continuous silhouette; gold center square large enough to survive at 16px.
Constraints: one H, two main colors, one gold square; no other details; no gradients, texture, outlines, shadows, dots, cells, networks, thin lines, 3D, text labels, mockup, or watermark.
Avoid: pixel-art noise, corporate monogram, sports team logo, circuit imagery, DNA, ornamental typography.
```
