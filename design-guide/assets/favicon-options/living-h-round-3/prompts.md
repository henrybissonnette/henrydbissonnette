## 01 — Macrocell colonies

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square favicon concept for HenryBissonnette.com, designed to retain its identity at 16x16 and 32x32
Primary request: Create an unmistakable uppercase H made from two compact living colonies of OVERSIZED MACRO-CELLS. The left upright is a tightly fused coral-red colony of only five or six large irregular round cells; the right upright is a tightly fused cobalt-violet colony of only five or six large irregular round cells. One thick warm-gold bridge cell joins the colonies at the optical center. From a distance the fused outer mass reads first as one bold H; only on closer view do the few large cell boundaries appear.
Input images: Image 1 is the original Living H and provides the cellular-colony idea; Image 2 is the selected Chromatic Morphogenesis website direction and provides the living-territory palette and spirit; Image 3 is the over-simplified split H and provides the required overall weight and tab-size silhouette, but restore some biological organization.
Scene/backdrop: perfectly quiet warm peach-cream square.
Style/medium: bold contemporary identity mark, flat screenprinted pigment with subtle handmade cellular boundaries, vector-friendly overall structure.
Composition/framing: centered H occupying about 82 percent of the square; very thick uprights; open top and bottom counters; compact margin.
Constraints: no more than thirteen major foreground regions total; cell shapes must be large enough to remain meaningful at 32px; H silhouette must dominate; one gold joining region; no tiny dots, no particles, no thin network lines, no text, no typography sample, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup, no watermark.
Avoid: bubble bath, grapes, molecule diagram, granular foam, decorative scatter, generic corporate monogram.
```

## 04 — Ecotone H

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square favicon concept for HenryBissonnette.com, designed to retain its identity at 16x16 and 32x32
Primary request: Create a clear heavy uppercase H that captures the site's CELL-TO-GRAPH ECOTONE. The left upright is one coral living tissue column divided into only five large irregular cellular chambers. Across the central golden bridge, the structure transforms into the right cobalt-violet upright: a single thick connected territory with only four large nodes and three broad connecting bands, all fused into a solid vertical silhouette. The transition must feel like nature becoming computation without either side breaking apart. At thumbnail scale it is simply a strong H; at larger scale the asymmetry reveals the metaphor.
Input images: Image 1 provides the Living H scaffold; Image 2 provides the original cell-to-network transition and vivid coral/gold/violet/cobalt palette; Image 3 provides the simplified silhouette target.
Scene/backdrop: flat warm peach-cream square.
Style/medium: editorial scientific emblem, flat graphic pigment, organic geometry disciplined into an icon.
Composition/framing: centered, wide heavy H occupying 82 percent of the square; short thick bridge; large negative-space counters; balanced visual mass.
Constraints: left side maximum five cell chambers; right side maximum four nodes and three thick bands; all elements must fuse into a stable H silhouette; no tiny nodes, no thin graph lines, no scattered particles, no annotations, no pointer marks, no text, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no mockup, no watermark.
Avoid: generic network logo, circuit board, molecule diagram, decorative complexity, literal brain, medical symbol.
```
