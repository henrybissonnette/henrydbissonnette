About

Curiosity, put to work.

I’m an engineer who enjoys understanding how things fit together, finding the simple shape inside a complicated problem, and building the result well.

How I approach the work

I care about the whole path from an early sketch to the person using the finished thing. That means asking good questions, making tradeoffs visible, and treating the small details as part of the design.

This site is a home for that work: practical projects, playful experiments, and notes from whatever I am learning next. It is deliberately simple today, with space to grow in directions I cannot predict yet.
